Section 01

About Us

Who We Are

GIVE HOPE PLEASE is a nonprofit organisation established to improve access to healthcare, rehabilitation, disability support, education and humanitarian assistance for people who may otherwise struggle to obtain the help they need.

Our approach brings together compassion and practical support. We aim to work with hospitals, healthcare professionals, rehabilitation providers, educational institutions, voluntary welfare organisations, community partners, donors and relevant agencies to identify needs and develop responsible pathways of assistance.

Why the Name 'GIVE HOPE PLEASE'?

Our name expresses both our purpose and the way we want to serve:

  • GIVE Represents action. Hope becomes meaningful when people are willing to contribute, serve, share and step forward to help.
  • HOPE Represents light during difficult circumstances - the possibility of healing, strength, opportunity, dignity and a better tomorrow.
  • PLEASE Represents humility, sincerity and respect. Charitable work should never diminish the dignity of the person receiving support.
Founder's Message

"Dear Friends, GIVE HOPE PLEASE began with a very simple thought: when someone is facing illness, disability, financial difficulty or uncertainty, even one act of support can restore hope."

"Through our years of working in healthcare and rehabilitation, we have seen how strongly a person's recovery can be influenced by access to timely care, professional guidance, family support and financial resources. We cannot solve every problem, but we can choose not to ignore the person in front of us."

— Founders, GIVE HOPE PLEASE

Our Vision

A society where people, regardless of financial circumstances or background, have greater opportunity to access appropriate healthcare, rehabilitation, education and support, enabling them to live with dignity and hope.

Our Mission

To give hope and create meaningful change by improving access to healthcare and rehabilitation, supporting people with disabilities and vulnerable communities, providing financial and educational assistance, and bringing individuals and organisations together to serve.
